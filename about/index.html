About

2026-08-06

Basement Uptime is notes on self-hosting and homelab operations: Proxmox, LXC, DNS, backups, and the undocumented gotchas that show up once you run this stuff long-term instead of following a setup tutorial once.

Posts come from real operational work. Something broke, here’s why, here’s the fix. No sponsored placement, no product seeded by a PR firm. A link to specific hardware or software means that’s what was actually used; some of those links may be affiliate links (see the footer).

Corrections and follow-up questions welcome.
