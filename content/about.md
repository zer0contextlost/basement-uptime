Basement Uptime is a small, focused set of notes on self-hosting and
homelab operations — Proxmox, LXC, DNS, backups, and the specific,
often-undocumented gotchas that show up when you actually run this stuff
long-term rather than just following a setup tutorial once.

Posts here come from real operational work: things that broke, why they
broke, and what fixed them. No sponsored placement, no product seeded by a
PR firm — where a link to a specific piece of hardware or software is
useful, it's linked because it's genuinely what was used, and some of
those links may be affiliate links (see the note in the footer).

Corrections and follow-up questions are welcome.
